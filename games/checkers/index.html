🔴 Jump the owl's pieces before it jumps yours!

Choose a level
👑 How to play: Move your red pieces one square diagonally forward. If you can hop over an owl piece into an empty space behind it, you must — and if that piece can keep jumping, keep going! Reach the far row and your piece becomes a king, free to move any diagonal way. Capture every owl piece — or leave the owl with no moves at all — to win.
