5️⃣ How to play: Gomoku is the old game of five in a row. You and the owl take turns putting one stone on any empty spot — stones stay where you put them. Line up five of yours in a row (across, up and down, or on a slant) before the owl does! You always go first. On Expert you can also capture the owl's stones.
✋ trap two of the owl's stones between yours to take them — five pairs wins
